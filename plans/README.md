  product; Stripe's own emails cover subscription invoices only. Notification
  infra (Novu + first-party inbox) exists and is wired to everything except
  purchases.

  collections with no reader; an ops email now fires per submission

- **Founding cap timing** (S): cap is checked at link creation, not at
  payment-landed; small overshoot window bounded by unpaid links. Largely

- **Direction options** (operator's call, from the audit's direction pass):
