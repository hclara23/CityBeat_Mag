## Summary

Audit CityBeat's public website, business directory, listing detail experience, claim/upgrade path, and owner dashboard. Identify conversion and retention improvements, emphasizing perks that reuse existing data and components without adding a recurring vendor or infrastructure charge.

## Scope

Reviewed the homepage commerce section, listing detail layouts, claim messaging, plan entitlements, owner/advertiser dashboards, leads, analytics, QR/review, badge, referral, content, and reporting capabilities. Deliver a prioritized product recommendation rather than modifying production code. Recommendations distinguish zero-new-monthly-cost reuse from items with possible marginal email, AI, moderation, or compute expense.

## Risks

Do not promise rankings, guaranteed leads, or unverifiable traffic. Keep Featured placement distinct from Premium card prominence. Avoid calling AI generation free because model usage can incur variable cost. Email reports, user notifications, PDFs, and human moderation can create marginal operating cost or labor even when no new subscription is required. Trust badges must reflect observable facts such as claimed ownership, freshness, response activity, or active offers.

## Verify Steps

1. Inspect the public homepage directory commerce section and listing detail actions. 2. Confirm existing Basic, Premium, and Featured entitlements in source. 3. Inspect owner dashboard components for analytics, leads, referrals, badges, AI assistance, deals, reviews, and reporting. 4. Rank recommendations by client value, implementation effort, and recurring-cost exposure. 5. Confirm every zero-cost recommendation can derive from existing first-party data or static templates.

## Rollback Plan

No product code, billing configuration, customer records, or external services are changed by this audit. If a recommendation is rejected, remove it from the future roadmap; no operational rollback is required.

## Notes

Key finding: the product already contains strong value—bilingual SEO pages, tracked calls/web/directions, quote leads, reviews and owner replies, QR tools, offers/posts/events, analytics/export, referral tools, badges, and owner reports—but these are fragmented across surfaces and under-explained on the homepage. Highest-value no-new-monthly-cost opportunities are a customer-actions ROI card, verified/fresh/responsive trust signals, a template-generated share kit and review QR poster, an onboarding checklist, a proof-led sales page, and eligibility for organic deal/editorial rotation without guaranteed placement.
