## Summary

Research the lawful and operationally sound payout cadence for connected editors and other payees, using current authoritative sources and Stripe Connect documentation.

## Context

CityBeat prefers editor commissions to leave as soon as cleared but is considering a predictable twice-monthly rule and needs to understand legal, contractual, and Stripe constraints.

## Scope

Compare after-clearance, twice-monthly, and monthly schedules under relevant New Mexico and United States rules, worker classification and contract considerations, Stripe Connect availability and payout controls, fees, reconciliation effort, and communication requirements. Provide citations and a recommendation.

## Risks

Worker classification and contract terms can change the applicable rules. Research is informational and must distinguish statutes or official guidance from inference; recommend qualified legal review where facts remain material.

## Verify Steps

Use primary authoritative sources where possible; record publication or access dates; confirm Stripe documentation is current; state assumptions and compare each cadence against compliance, cost, cash-flow, and reconciliation criteria.

## Rollback Plan

No system change is permitted. Withdraw or amend recommendations if newer authority or corrected classification facts change the analysis.

## Notes

Research does not authorize a payout-schedule change. Configuration depends on both this recommendation and the forensic audit.
